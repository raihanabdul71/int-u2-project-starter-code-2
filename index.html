Help Restore Our Forests: Wild Fire Prevention


Why should you care!

Uncontrolled fires causes devastation, loss of life and property, destroy natural habitats, and release dangerous smoke that degrades air quality. It is important to recognize that it isn't just single individuals these fires hurt, but entire communities and ecosystems. With the help of community and the government we should be able to improve the health of our planet. Want to be of help in growing more trees to support our environments? Donate here.

Severe Health Risks

Wildfire smoke creates a toxic mixture of pollutants and fine matter. The microscopic particles can penetrate into the lungs and seep into the bloodstream, triggering asthma attacks and heart attacks.

Infrastructure and Economic Devastation

Causes the destruction of critical infrastructure such as homes, power grids, and communication lines. This cost billions of dollars annually for firefighting efforts, property loss, and lost economic productivity.

YOU CAN ALSO HELP PREVENT WILDFIRES!

There are many ways you can take on the act on making a diffrence yourself. Here are a few ways you can help your ecosystem:

  • Extinguishing a camp fire
  • Keeping your vehicle maintained to prevent sparks
  • It is also important to avoid any activities that involve sparks when it is hot, dry, or windy.
  • Conditions and local restrictions should guide your decision for any fire-related activity.
